Six Major Functions
1. Job Post Approvals
Assigned to: De Silva P.K.N (IT24102131)
User: Recruitment Manager
Function: Allows hiring managers to create, publish, update, or remove job listings.
Expected Outcome: Job listings are made visible to job seekers in real-time.
CRUD operations:
Create: Approve and publish a new job post.
Read: View pending, approved, and rejected job posts.
Update: Change approval status (e.g., approve → reject).
Delete: Remove an approved job post from listings
2. Candidate Interview Schedules
Assigned to: Kuruppuarachchige K.A.H.B (IT24102046)
User: HR Assistant
Function: Enables HR staff to search candidate profiles using filters such as skill, education,
and location.
Expected Outcome: HR can efficiently generate lists of qualified applicants.
CRUD operations:
Create: Add new interview schedule for a candidate.
Read: View all upcoming and past interviews.
Update: Change interview date, time, or interviewer.
Delete: Cancel an interview schedule.

6. Job Postings
Assigned to: Inshaf M.J.M (IT24100618)
User: Company Hiring Manager
Function: Creates, edits, and removes job postings they have published in the system.
Expected Outcome: Job listings remain accurate, updated, and relevant for potential

7. Minor Functions
• Upload and update profile photos

8. System Limitations / Constraints
• The system will be optimized for desktop browsers only, with minimal mobile
support.
• No email, SMS, or third-party integration features in this phase.
• File size for resumes will be restricted to 2MB.
• All user and job data will be stored in structured text files using Java I/O.

10. Conclusion
LankaJobsHub.com is designed to meet the growing need for an efficient and accessible
digital recruitment platform in Sri Lanka. The system aims to modernize traditional hiring
methods by reducing dependency on paper-based processes and fragmented job postings. By
offering an all-in-one platform for job seekers and employers, the system delivers real-world
functionality and academic relevance.
Our development approach emphasizes modularity, secure Java-based implementation, and
real-time interaction capabilities. With well-defined user roles and clearly assigned functions,
each team member will contribute to the successful and timely delivery of the platform. This
project not only aligns with the objectives of the SE2030 module but also reflects our
commitment to practical software engineering practices. The system has significant potential
for real-world application and can be further enhanced in future iterations.
